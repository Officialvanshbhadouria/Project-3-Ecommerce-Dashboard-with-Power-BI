<strong>E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | Power BI</strong>

Built an interactive E-Commerce Sales Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales, profit, products, customers, regions, and payment methods.

Key Features <br>
📊 Sales, Profit, Quantity & Order KPIs<br>
🛍️ Product and Category Performance Analysis<br>
👥 Customer & Repeat Customer Analysis<br>
🌍 State-wise Sales Performance<br>
💳 Payment Mode & Profit Analysis<br>
📈 Monthly Sales & Profit Trends<br>
🔎 Top Products and Top Customers<br>
💡 Business Insights & Strategic Recommendations<br>
Tools & Skills<br>
<br>
Power BI | DAX | Data Modeling | Data Visualization | Business Intelligence | Data Analysis
<br>
Key Insights
<br>
The analysis identified Electronics as the highest-performing category, while Beauty and Grocery showed comparatively lower performance. Maharashtra generated the highest sales among states, while customer and payment-mode analysis provided additional opportunities for business optimization.<br>
<br>
Project Goal:
To transform raw e-commerce data into an interactive dashboard that helps businesses understand performance, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.
